Trucks and drivers for hire

Our tipper hire service in detail: how it works, how you can hire and what to expect.

Tipper hire

A tipper truck carries loose material and lifts its tray to unload on the spot, which makes it the workhorse of any site. We supply the truck and an experienced driver, so you do not have to manage licences or servicing.

Hire it by the hour for a quick job, by the day for steady work, or for the length of a project.

Best for

Driveways, trenches, small civil works and any job where material is moved and tipped in more than one place.

What you get

  • Truck and driver supplied together
  • Flexible hire periods
  • Loads tipped exactly where you need them

Hire options

By the hour

A simple choice for short jobs and single loads.

By the day

Suits steady work across a full day, such as moving spoil or delivering several loads.

For a project

Plan a truck across the length of a job, with times agreed up front.

How hire works

  1. Tell us the job, the material and the site address.
  2. We confirm the truck, the driver and the start time.
  3. The driver arrives, loads or tips as directed, and the job is done.

Materials, delivered

The sand, stone and soil behind good building and landscaping work, brought to your site.

Aggregates delivery

Aggregates are the bulk materials behind foundations, drives and paths: sand, gravel, crushed rock and road base. We collect from the supplier and deliver to your site in one trip, so you do not have to arrange both.

Each material has a job. Sand beds pavers and pipes, gravel suits drainage and driveways, and crushed rock or road base forms a firm layer under concrete and roads.

Best for

Builders, landscapers and homeowners who need the right material on site when the work is ready for it.

What you get

  • Sand, gravel, crushed rock and road base
  • Collection and delivery in a single trip
  • Material tipped where you ask

Soil and landscaping supplies

Healthy gardens start with the right soil. We deliver topsoil for lawns and garden beds, mulch to protect plants and hold moisture, and general fill to level or raise ground.

Because loads are tipped from the truck, material lands close to where it will be spread, which saves carrying it by hand.

Best for

New lawns, garden beds, raised areas and levelling work.

What you get

  • Topsoil for lawns and gardens
  • Mulch for beds and borders
  • Fill for levelling and raising ground

Spoil and rubble removal

Digging, demolition and renovation leave material that has to go somewhere. We load and carry away excavation spoil, concrete and brick rubble and general site waste, so your site stays clear and safe to work on.

Regular pick-ups stop piles building up, and a clear site makes the next stage of work faster.

Best for

Excavations, demolitions, renovations and end-of-job clean-ups.

What you get

  • Excavation spoil taken away
  • Concrete, brick and rubble removal
  • Site clean-ups

Bulk and general freight

When the load is big, larger trucks make more sense. Our heavy trucks and truck-and-dog combinations carry large volumes in a single run, so you need fewer trips and less time on the road.

This suits major earthworks and large deliveries where volume matters more than tight access.

Best for

Large earthworks, quarry runs and bigger civil projects.

What you get

  • Heavy trucks and dog trailers
  • Large volumes per trip
  • Runs planned around site access

Scheduled haulage

If you need the same run again and again, we can set it up as a regular service. Loads leave at agreed times, drivers get to know your site, and you have one point of contact for any change.

Regular haulage keeps a project on programme, because trucks arrive when your crew is ready for them.

Best for

Builders and civil contractors with ongoing work.

What you get

  • Agreed days and times
  • Drivers who know your site
  • One contact for changes
